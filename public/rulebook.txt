









IEEE CS × IEEE CIS HACKATHON

TWO-REVIEW RULEBOOK

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Chennai  •  14 October 2026  •  8:30 AM–4:30 PM

1. AT A GLANCE

Format: Offline, one-day hackathon.

Team size: 2–4 members.

Expected participation: Minimum 25 teams.

IEEE membership: Not required unless stated in the final registration notice.

Registration: Team formation and registration must be completed before event day.

Problem statements: Released at the opening.

2. BUILD REQUIREMENT

Required: A functional full-stack prototype with a meaningful AI / Computational Intelligence component.

Integration: Frontend + backend + database/API + AI/CI workflow should work together.

Parallel work: AI and full-stack development may happen in parallel.

Resources: Pretrained models, APIs, public datasets, frameworks and model-building resources are allowed.

Scope: A working prototype is sufficient; production-scale engineering is not required.

3. AI / DEVELOPMENT TOOLS

Allowed: ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, GitHub Copilot, AI coding agents, Lovable, Bolt, Hugging Face, TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost, model APIs, vector databases, public datasets and equivalent resources.

Advanced / premium models: Allowed unless specifically placed on the Organizing Committee's restricted list.

AI-generated code: Must be understood, reviewed and defensible by the team.

Not allowed: Complete pre-built solutions, previous hackathon projects submitted as new work, plagiarism, stolen credentials/data or deliberate copying.

4. TEAM & RESOURCE RULES

Templates / libraries / frameworks: Allowed.

Existing code: General-purpose components are allowed; a complete pre-developed solution for the chosen problem is not.

Licensing: Teams are responsible for permitted use of third-party code, datasets, APIs, models and assets.

Day scholars: If leaving at or before 3:30 PM, at least one hostel/residential student from the team must remain through final evaluation and represent the team.

Breaks: Teams may continue working during tea/lunch breaks if permitted by the venue/Organizing Committee.

5. SUBMISSION

Required: GitHub repository with source code and a clear README.

Deployment: Working deployment link where available; localhost/demo setup is acceptable when deployment is impractical.

Deadline: Submit before the final review begins.

Evaluation readiness: All members should be able to explain the architecture, AI/CI component, integration and key implementation choices.
